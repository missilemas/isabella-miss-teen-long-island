A Note from Isabella

Hi everyone! I'm Isabella Simon, and I'm competing for Miss Long Island Teen 2027 on Sunday, November 1.

My platform is Beyond the Label: seeing the person, not their diagnosis. The money raised through my platform goes to the John Theissen Children's Foundation in Wantagh, which has helped sick and underprivileged kids across Long Island since 1992 with holiday toys, rides to and from treatments, help with medical bills, and a free Family Fun Center where kids can just be kids.

Every People's Choice vote helps me support them, so I'd be so grateful for yours. Thank you so much for your support! 💕
